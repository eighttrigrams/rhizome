# Rhizome - User Manual

## Features

Rhizome has many features which are not obvious
to find. Here's a small listing

### Linking to other Items from the description of one Item

Put `[<id-of-other-item](link text)` into the markdown text  
of one Item and it becomes a clickable link to that other Item.
The other Item will become the selected item, and `option-b` will
bring you back to the original Item.

### YouTube videos support

#### Subscribing to YouTube channels (feed)

Press shift+option+s to reach the configuration view  
(where you can also add atom feed subscriptions).

#### QR Codes

YouTube videos will be presented with a QR code below them,  
which allows you to jump to that video with your phone.

#### The floating player

A video does not play in the Item any more. In its place there is  
a still with a play button on it. Clicking that starts the video in  
one player, floating above the app, which keeps playing while you  
edit, search, or move to another Item entirely.

Drag it by the strip along its top to move it between the four  
corners. It goes away when you press the X on it, or when you start  
another video, which replaces it. There is only ever one.

The player carries a QR code of its own, for whatever it is playing.  
The one below a still is about that Item's video; the one on the  
player is about what you are watching, and once you have moved on  
those are no longer the same video.

### Hierarchy mode

Access with shift+option+h.
